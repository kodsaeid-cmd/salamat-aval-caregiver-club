# RBAC + Contract Lifecycle V2

Acceptance contract:

- Users/access shows role presets and every module from both STAFF and CAREGIVER catalogs.
- Selecting a role preset replaces the visible matrix; per-user checkbox overrides are saved independently.
- Contract case is reconciled automatically from IN_CONTRACT job applications.
- Contract registry shows unique number, ad-derived title, caregiver, rank stars, salary, dates, progress, remaining days and renewal proximity.
- Contract case has tabs for base data, dispatch/providers, supervision with 10,000-character audited notes, and finance/credit data.
- Dispatch/provider rows open the canonical four-tab caregiver scorecard.
- Contract supervision and financial writes remain protected by staff.contracts update permission.
- Schema additions are additive and do not alter historical evaluation/point ledgers.
