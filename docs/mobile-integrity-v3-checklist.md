## Mobile acceptance checklist

- Dashboard tab opens the dashboard.
- Users and access tab opens `کاربران و دسترسی‌ها` for admin users.
- Other primary tabs open their real authorized modules.
- More opens the complete authorized module list.
- Pointer, click and keyboard activation work.
- Rapid taps are queued rather than discarded.
- Backdrop, inert and scroll locks are cleared after navigation.
- Mobile tables remain readable cards after every render.
- Active tab and `aria-current` stay synchronized.
- Eden Green is dominant and Red Alert is limited to accents and warnings.
- Desktop above 760px remains unchanged.
