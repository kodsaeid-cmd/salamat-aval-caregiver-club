# Referral rewards V2

- Existing 200,000 and 300,000 toman referral rewards remain unchanged.
- A caregiver may submit a 3,000,000 toman referral benefit request after 10 confirmed referred caregivers join the network.
- A caregiver may submit an 8,000,000 toman referral benefit request after at least 10 confirmed referred caregivers join the network and at least 7 of them have reached IN_CONTRACT.
- Requests require an explicit authorized staff decision; the application does not automatically approve requests.
- Request history and decision history must be retained permanently.
